Corporate Website Redesign for a Digital Entertainment Company

Summary

The client, a leading digital entertainment company specializing in games and creative content, wanted to redesign its corporate website to attract new talent and present its expanding creative vision.

Over time, recruitment information had become scattered across departments, and the corporate site no longer reflected the company’s scale or energy. Our mission was to create a unified platform that communicated the company’s culture, showcased its achievements, and strengthened its recruitment and sales presence.

The project evolved in three phases:

  1. Redesigning the Recruitment Page to convey authenticity and excitement
  2. Revamping the Top Page to communicate brand identity
  3. Building a Work Archive that beautifully showcases the company’s creative output

In addition, a presentation deck was designed to align the website with the company’s sales strategy and external communications.

My Contributions

As Lead Web Designer & UX Planner, I:

  • Defined overall site structure and page hierarchy
  • Conducted competitive and user research
  • Designed layouts, visuals, and responsive components
  • Directed interview photography and content production
  • Collaborated with marketing and HR teams to align site goals

Outcome

The redesign delivered tangible results. Within months, the site saw a 200% increase in inquiries, while internal teams reported smoother communication and faster hiring decisions. Beyond numbers, the most meaningful outcome was that the website finally reflected the company’s identity — a creative organization that celebrates individuality, collaboration, and growth. Through this project, I learned the power of structure and storytelling in digital communication — how design can not only organize information but also shape emotion and connection.

Recruitment Page

The Recruitment Page was designed to humanize the company and create an emotional connection between potential applicants and the people who already work there. Instead of a transactional “job listing” experience, the page invites users to discover the company’s culture through stories and visuals.

To achieve that, I emphasized visual hierarchy and emotional storytelling. The layout begins with an impactful hero section that features portraits of employees layered over soft glass textures and pastel gradients. This not only makes the visuals approachable but also subtly communicates the openness of the company culture.

The page adopted glassmorphism, inspired by Apple’s macOS design language, reflecting creativity and trend sensitivity. A pastel color palette ensured visual harmony while symbolizing the company’s diversity. Each section used distinct rhythms in layout to maintain engagement as users scroll.

Content Structure & Features

  • Hero Visual

    A visual introduction showing real employees, paired with an authentic tagline expressing company culture and mission. The transparent layers and soft colors evoke inclusivity and warmth.

  • About the Company

    A concise narrative describing the company’s philosophy, core values, and creative environment. Rather than using corporate jargon, this section uses approachable language and a friendly tone.

  • Pick-Up Positions

    A carousel or card layout that highlights in-demand roles, with each card linking directly to a detailed job description. The section also functions as a motivational entry point for users unfamiliar with the company’s structure.

  • Interview Integration

    A key storytelling component where department heads and team members share their work experiences. Each interview includes:
    Team overview and role description
    Personal motivation and work challenges
    Advice or message to future applicants
    The layout alternates image placement (left/right) to maintain visual rhythm while scrolling.

  • Application Flow

    Clear CTA buttons and progress indicators for application submission, ensuring users understand each step from reading the job description to final application.

    A visually lighter section introducing mentorship programs, skill-development opportunities, and the company’s approach to nurturing young creators.

Top Page

The Top Page serves as the digital front door of the company. Its goal was twofold: communicate the company’s creative identity to the public and act as a strategic touchpoint for both recruitment and business inquiries.

The previous top page lacked structure and content depth. In collaboration with the marketing team, I reorganized content around the company’s expanding studios and business capabilities, positioning the site as both a brand showcase and a sales tool.

Balancing entertainment vibrancy with corporate clarity, I designed a dynamic yet business-friendly visual tone. A slideshow of in-house creators’ works served as the key visual, presenting the company as both a creative powerhouse and a professional partner.

Content Structure & Features

  • Hero Visual Slider

    A dynamic carousel of featured projects created by the in-house studios. It immediately communicates creative credibility and aesthetic quality, using smooth transitions and contextual captions.

  • Corporate Message

    A concise yet emotionally resonant tagline introducing the company’s vision and philosophy. Positioned beneath the hero for maximum visibility, it creates an immediate emotional connection.

  • Service & Studio Overview

    A modular grid introducing each studio’s area of expertise — from game production to digital content creation. Each block links to detailed pages, allowing users to explore seamlessly.

  • Related Services, Blog & News Section

    Introduces affiliated programs like event production, training academies, and partnership initiatives. Using colorful icons and cards, this section helps express the company’s diversity.A live-updating feed showing the latest activities, interviews, and announcements. This feature keeps the website dynamic and encourages repeat visits.

Work Archive

Improve accessibility and management of studio achievements

Before the redesign, each of the nine studios maintained its own “Works” section, resulting in inconsistent presentation and a fragmented user experience. The goal was to create a unified archive that showcases all projects while enabling users to explore intuitively.

The key design philosophy was “curated simplicity.” The archive behaves like a digital gallery — clean, image-focused, and flexible across devices.

The archive emphasizes visual storytelling — large imagery, clear hierarchy, and minimalist framing — maintaining consistency with the top page tone while ensuring projects remain the focus.

Features

  • Unified Project Grid

    The landing view presents all projects in a clean, masonry-like grid that automatically arranges items. The design emphasizes images first, with titles appearing on hover for a minimalist feel.

    Filtering System

    Users can refine results by category. Filtering happens dynamically to keep users engaged.

    Project Detail Pages

    Each project page includes, Large hero image or video, Project overview and related studio information.

    Studio Integration

    From each project, users can directly jump to its associated studio page, reinforcing brand unity and increasing content circulation.

    CMS Management (WordPress Integration)

    The system was designed to be intuitive, allowing quick uploads without compromising layout quality.

Result

After launch, the redesign led to:

  • Improved applicant experience with consolidated recruitment info
  • Stronger internal collaboration between departments
  • A flexible, scalable web structure aligned with the company’s growth
