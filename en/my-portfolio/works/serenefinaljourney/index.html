Cremation facilities for a serene final journey

Assignment work in Kyoto University of Arts

Summary

While various challenges are observed due to an ageing and multi-death society, death is avoided and no positive solutions have been outwardly addressed. We choose where we work, where we live and where we travel to on holiday. As an extension of this, we should be able to “choose where we will be mourned”. Providing the departed with the peace of mind that funeral arrangements are made before death, and time for the bereaved family to heal their grief, death is not avoided as a tragedy, but accepted positively as a part of life. The aim is to create a space that proposes such new values.
