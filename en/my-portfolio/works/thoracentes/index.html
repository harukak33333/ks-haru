Thoracentes, Inc.

Branding Design for a University-based Medical Startup

Summary

This project involved a comprehensive branding design for a university-based medical startup specializing in lung surgery innovation.

From defining the mission and values to creating the logo, website, and business cards, I led the design direction end-to-end in close collaboration with a marketing planner.

Our goal was to visualize the company’s pioneering spirit in the medical field through a unified and trustworthy brand identity.

My Contributions

As the lead designer, I oversaw the project from strategy to final delivery:

  • Facilitated workshops and interviews with the founder to define the mission.
  • Directed logo concept development, from symbolic ideation to final refinement and color selection.
  • Designed and built the corporate website using WordPress, implementing multilingual and blog features.
  • Supervised printing and material direction for business cards, ensuring color consistency across media.
  • Delivered comprehensive brand guidelines to ensure consistent future communication.

Through hands-on involvement in every phase, I ensured that each design decision contributed to a cohesive and credible brand experience, helping the startup gain confidence and presence in both medical and business communities.

Outcome

Through cohesive strategy, design, and implementation, the new branding established a trustworthy and forward-thinking image for the startup. It successfully translated medical expertise and innovation into a visual identity that resonates with both local and global audiences, forming a solid foundation for future growth.

Brand Strategy – Defining the Mission

I began with workshops and interviews to define the mission and values. My goal was to understand how the founder wanted to position the technology — not just technically, but emotionally.

Key Strategy Features

  • Conducted interviews to translate the founder’s philosophy into clear, communicable brand values.
  • Developed mission and value statements emphasizing trust, precision, and innovation in medicine.
  • Established a verbal identity that could guide all visual and communication touchpoints.

Logo Design – Visualizing Innovation and Trust

The next step was to design a logo that could be used across various contexts — from websites and presentations to medical devices and printed materials.

We explored symbolic forms that reflected both the company’s medical focus and its vision for future technology: lungs, lenses, AR interfaces, tubes, snakes (symbol of medicine), and the initial letter of the company name.

Around 20 rough concepts were developed, refined into five finalists, and tested internally.

The final mark combined the imagery of a lung and a lens, representing both the company’s expertise in pulmonary surgery and its innovative, forward-looking perspective.

Key Design Features

  • Symbol + logotype format for flexible use across digital and physical media.
  • Grid-based construction ensured clarity and reproducibility at any scale.
  • Designed to convey both scientific precision and human warmth, avoiding a cold, overly clinical impression.
  • The logo motif combines the “Lens”, which allows the patient's body to be seen through AR technology, and the “Lung”, which is the President's area of expertise.
  • The logotype is based on ‘Vox’, which has a soft and angular shape like a drainage tube, giving an impression of robotics.

Website Design – Simplicity that Builds Trust

The website was designed and developed to communicate reliability and clarity, crucial for a medical startup seeking to earn the trust of doctors, investors, and research partners.

The visual identity extended from the chosen logo color palette, creating a consistent tone across all digital touchpoints. Motion and animation were minimized to maintain focus and credibility, while a multilingual structure supported future global expansion.

Content Structure and Features

  • Simple, confidence-building layout: Clean structure with ample whitespace for easy information scanning.
  • Language switch feature: Enabled seamless transition between Japanese and English to reach international audiences.
  • WordPress implementation: Empowered the client team to post articles and updates without technical support.
  • Minimal motion: Subtle transitions used only to guide attention, maintaining a calm, professional atmosphere.

Business Card Design – Compact, Memorable, and Bilingual

The business card design adopted a horizontal layout with Japanese and English on opposite sides, supporting bilingual communication in professional settings.

The logo symbol was featured prominently, leaving a lasting visual impression that matched the clarity and professionalism of the brand.

Key Design Features

  • Large, centered logo symbol emphasizing the brand’s identity at first glance.
  • Double-sided bilingual design for international networking.
  • Matte finish and subtle embossing to add tactile quality and precision.
  • Alignment and type hierarchy designed for effortless readability.

Reflection

This project reminded me of the importance of translating specialized knowledge into accessible, emotional design.
In medical fields, visual communication often leans too heavily on precision and seriousness, but real trust is built through warmth and clarity.
Balancing these qualities—science and empathy—was the most challenging and rewarding part of the process.

Working directly with a surgeon-founder also sharpened my ability to listen deeply, interpret abstract ideas, and guide them into tangible design outputs.
The experience strengthened my confidence in bridging different disciplines—medicine, business, and design—and reaffirmed my belief that branding is not just about aesthetics, but about helping ideas earn credibility in the world.
