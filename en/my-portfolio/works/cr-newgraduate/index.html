C&R Graduate Recruitment Website

Redesigning a page to improve the application experience for new graduates

Summary

This project focused on redesigning a graduate recruitment website to better communicate the company’s identity, clarify job roles, and improve the application experience for new graduates.

The existing page struggled to clearly convey who the company was, what roles were available, and why students should apply. Messaging was overly verbose, navigation to the application page was complex, and the content failed to resonate with the intended audience of young graduates and specialist-track candidates.

Through UX-driven restructuring and visual redesign, the renewed page delivers clear messaging, intuitive navigation, and content tailored to the mindset and expectations of new graduates, while also aiming to increase conversion rates.

My Contributions

As the UI/UX Designer on this project, I collaborated closely with a UX Designer and took responsibility for the visual and interaction design.

  • Proposed UX improvement points based on analysis of the existing page
  • Designed the full page layout and visual system based on the new information architecture
  • Created interaction designs, including key visuals and hover animations for buttons and UI elements

Outcome

The renewed recruitment page presents a clearer, more engaging narrative for new graduates, helping them quickly understand the company, envision their role, and confidently proceed to application. By aligning content structure, visual design, and interaction patterns with user intent, the project strengthened both employer branding and usability, while supporting higher engagement and conversion.

Understanding the Challenge

An audit of the existing recruitment page revealed several key issues:

  • Core messages were lengthy and unfocused, making it difficult to understand what the company wanted to communicate
  • The company’s overall business and mission were unclear
  • Job descriptions lacked clarity, making it hard for applicants to imagine actual responsibilities
  • The path to the application page was complicated and unintuitive
  • Employee interviews featured mostly senior, generalist staff, making it difficult for new graduates or specialist-focused candidates to relate

These issues reduced both engagement and motivation to apply.

Solution & Key Improvements

  • 1. Simplified messaging

    Key messages were rewritten and paired with an abstract visual representation of the company mission to communicate the company overview concisely.

  • 2. Business model clarity

    A dedicated section was added to explain business domains and how the company creates value.

  • 3. Improved application flow

    Clear calls-to-action were placed immediately after job descriptions to streamline the path to application and improve conversion.

  • 4. Career fit support

    A simple career-matching quiz was introduced to help users explore suitable roles across a wide range of job categories.

  • 5. Work culture visualization

    Workplace features and benefits were expressed through data and numbers to make the working environment more tangible.

  • 6. Relatable storytelling

    Navigation was added to interviews with young specialist employees to foster empathy and relatability with the target audience.
