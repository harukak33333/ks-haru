Yu-char-gee

Making Charging Fun for Yuuchami

Summary

“Yu-char-gee” is a playful, habit-forming app designed to help Yuuchami, a cheerful model and TV personality, manage one of her few everyday struggles — charging her phone.

Our team developed this project in just six weeks, covering everything from user research and problem definition to concept ideation, design, and implementation.

The project was showcased at GIFTech 2024 Spring, where our team won first place for strong ownership and innovative design.

My Contributions

As the UI/UX Designer, I led the research synthesis, concept design, and visual design, collaborating closely with engineers and project managers to shape an app that reflects Yuuchami’s bright personality and lifestyle.

Outcome

“Yu-char-gee” transformed the mundane act of charging into a source of joy and self-motivation. By combining behavioral psychology, playful UI, and personal insight, we created a product that fits seamlessly into Yuuchami’s life and personality.

Process

  • Conducted user interviews with Yuuchami and her management team
  • Identified core behavioral patterns and motivational triggers
  • Defined concept through empathy mapping and problem reframing
  • Designed interaction flows and gamified feedback loops
  • Delivered full visual design and prototype for demo presentation

Understanding the Challenge

Our first and biggest challenge came during the interview phase — Yuuchami confidently told us she had no problems at all. She’s known for her super-positive mindset and ability to see everything in a good light.

However, through deeper conversations, one charming truth emerged: Yuuchami described herself as “lazy” — so much so that she often asks her manager or mother to charge her phone for her. That became our insight.

Instead of/assets/imgething to make her life easier, what if we designed a product that encourages her to take small actions herself, while making it fun and effortless? This became the core concept: “Helping others starts with helping yourself.”

Design Question

Once we had our direction, the next question was:
How do we get Yuuchami to charge her phone on her own initiative?

She told us the main reason she avoids charging is simple — it’s too much hassle to get up and walk to the charger. So, we explored ways to turn charging into a playful, rewarding experience, something she’d look forward to rather than avoid.

Key Features

  • 1. Smart Charging Reminder

    Using lo/assets/imge app sends a friendly “Time to charge!” notification whenever Yuuchami approaches her home. Once she connects her charger, she can draw a digital fortune slip (omikuji) — inspired by her love of horoscopes and fortune-telling.

  • 2. Interactive Charging Widget

    /assets/img We designed an animated character widget that visually represents her phone’s battery level. The character lives in a “cyber world,” turning cute and lively when charging, but gradually fading as the battery drops.

  • 3. Manager Connection via LINE

    We implemented a LINE integration that allows her battery level to be shared with her manager’s account. This ensures reminders reach her before her phone dies.

  • 4. Character Collection Screen

    We created a “Character List” feature where users can view and collect generated characters. This turned charging into a playful, game-like experience.

Reflection

This project taught me how powerful emotional design can be — especially when solving everyday “non-problems.” Designing “Yu-char-gee” reminded me that UX isn’t just about solving pain points — it’s about creating positive emotional impact that makes users smile.
