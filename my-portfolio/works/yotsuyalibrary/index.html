Project overview

Wakaba, Shinjuku, used to be called Samegawabashi, where a poor neighbourhood was formed. Today there is no favela here, but it has been left behind by urban development and has become a densely built-up area. Due to high land prices and good transport links, there is a large influx of new residents from outside the area. On this land, I planned a community centre that would deepen the bonds of the local community. The aim is to create a place where people with different attributes, including new residents, old residents and outsiders just passing through, can interact with each other through an open space that anyone can use.
